Mathematics and numeracy

The mathematics and numeracy learning domain focuses on children's ability to understand, describe, discuss, and think about what they see in their environment. This domain outlines the development of foundational concepts needed for using numbers meaningfully, such as counting and understanding quantity. It supports the use of rich, specific language to talk and think about time, length, distance, capacity, and area, integrating these concepts into everyday activities.
