Social and emotional learning

Social and Emotional (SEL) skills help children build self-awareness, develop positive relationships, and navigate everyday challenges. Developing strong social and emotional skills enable children to thrive in their learning environment, leading to better holistic wellbeing. Social and emotional learning refers to two groups of closely related skills that help shape who children are, how they manage emotions and thoughts and how they communicate and relate to others while exploring the world around them. These skills are essential for children to have a sense of belonging, being and becoming as outlined in the EYLF V2.0.
