Learning domains

The KLPT focuses on 5 priority learning domains aligned with the Early Years Learning Framework V2.0 and the Queensland kindergarten learning guideline QKLG 2024. The domains highlight core skills that can be observed through everyday play, interactions and experiences, helping teachers and educators gather evidence, recognise children's strengths and inform responsive planning and assessment.
