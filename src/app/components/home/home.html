Introduction to the KLPT

The Kindergarten Learning Progression Toolkit (KLPT) is a Queensland Government initiative that provides teachers and educators with a voluntary, formative, child-centred assessment toolkit.

The KLPT supports teachers and educators to use high-quality assessment practices to build children's capabilities across five priority learning domains that align with the Early Years Learning Framework V2.0 and the Queensland kindergarten learning guideline (QKLG).

Watch the video below to explore the features of the KLPT.

The strengths-based KLPT recognises and values children’s diverse ways of knowing, being and doing, including the many ways children express themselves, connect with others and make meaning. The KLPT acknowledges Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander worldviews, and centres the importance of culture, traditions, family and kinship in a child’s learning. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander perspectives are intentionally embedded throughout the toolkit.

The KLPT is designed to support inclusive, strengths-based teaching, not to compare or rank children.

Teachers and educators can use it to:

  • reflect on their practice and pedagogy
  • recognise and build on each child's strengths
  • intentionally plan meaningful, inclusive and responsive learning experiences
  • celebrate growth over time, in ways that honour identity, culture and community
  • engage in professional conversations using a shared language for children's strengths and learning
